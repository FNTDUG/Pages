Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not intentionally collect personal details such as your name or email address. However, like virtually all websites, some information is collected automatically when you visit:

  • IP address. Your device's IP address is collected and processed by our hosting/content-delivery provider and by the advertising and analytics services we use (see below). This is standard for websites and is used to deliver the site to you, keep it secure, and serve advertising.
  • Device and browsing information. Such as your browser type, operating system, device type, screen size, the page that referred you, and the pages you view on the site.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. Small files stored by your browser, used primarily by our advertising partner (see "Cookies and advertising" below).

How your information is shared

We share the automatically-collected information described above — including your IP address — with the third-party services that make the site work. We do this so the site can load, stay secure, and display ads:

  • Cloudflare provides our hosting, content delivery, and security. Cloudflare receives and processes your IP address and request data to serve the site and protect it against abuse. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense serves the advertisements on the site. Google and its partners may use your IP address, cookies, and device information to serve and personalize ads and to measure their performance.

We do not sell your personal information. We only share the data needed to operate the site and display advertising as described here.

Cookies and advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or browse in private/incognito mode. Blocking cookies may change the ads you see but will not break the guide content.

Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience gaming guide and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live — for example, the EEA/UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA — you may have rights regarding your data, such as the right to access, delete, or object to certain processing. Because we do not hold user accounts, most of the data involved is handled by our advertising and hosting providers, and you can exercise your choices through Google Ad Settings and your browser's cookie controls. To make a request or ask a question, contact us using the details below.

Data retention

We do not maintain our own database of visitors. Automatically-generated logs (for example, via Cloudflare) and advertising/analytics data are retained by those providers in accordance with their own policies.
