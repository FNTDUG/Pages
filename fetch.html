How it works

Three steps from your first receipt to a gift card code.

Almost any receipt earns points, from any store.

  1. Open Fetch and tap the scan button at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Lay the receipt flat in good light, with the store name, date and total in the photo. Long receipts can be scanned in parts.
  3. Check the photo is sharp, then submit. Points usually arrive within minutes.
  4. Scan soon after you shop. Fetch only accepts recent receipts, and the app shows the time limit.

Do

  • Scan your own shopping, or your household's with permission.
  • Scan each receipt once.
  • Stay within the weekly limit shown in the app.

Don't

  • Scan receipts you found, were sent, or got online.
  • Edit or fake anything on a receipt.
  • Scan a receipt twice or on two accounts. Fetch can take points back or ban you.

Reach milestones in games for much bigger point drops.

  1. Open the Play tab in Fetch and pick a game, like one of the featured games above.
  2. Install it from the link inside Fetch, or your progress may not count.
  3. Open it once from Fetch, then play. Each milestone has a time limit shown in the app.
  4. Points arrive automatically. Keep the game installed until they do.

Good to know

  • You never need to spend money to reach a milestone.
  • Games and points can differ in your app. The app always has the final say.
  • Points can take up to a day to arrive after a milestone.

Swap your points for a gift card, delivered as a digital code.

  1. Open the Rewards tab in Fetch and pick a gift card, like Roblox.
  2. Choose an amount and confirm. Fetch shows the points cost before you agree.
  3. Your code appears in Fetch, usually within minutes, and stays saved there.
  4. Redeem it on the official site. For Roblox, sign in at roblox.com/redeem.

Keep your code safe

  • Never share it. Anyone who sees it can use it.
  • Nobody legitimate will ever ask for your code.
  • "Free Robux" sites and generators are scams.

Questions

My receipt was rejected

Retake the photo in better light with the store name, date and total visible. Receipts older than the app allows, or already scanned, can't be accepted.

I reached a milestone but got no points

Points can take a while. Reopen Fetch, keep the game installed and wait a day. If they still haven't arrived, contact Fetch support from the help section in the app.

My code doesn't work

Make sure you're on the official redeem page, signed in to the right account, and copied the code exactly. If it still fails, contact Fetch support from the app.

Can the FNTD User Guide fix my Fetch account?

No. We can't see or change Fetch accounts, points or codes. Only Fetch support can help.
