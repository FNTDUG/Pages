QUEST 1SCAN RECEIPTS

Almost any receipt earns points, from any store, even if you didn't buy a featured brand.

OBJECTIVES
REWARDPOINTS FOR EVERY RECEIPT
DO
  • Scan your own shopping, or your household's with permission.
  • Scan each receipt once, then bin it or keep it safe.
  • Watch the weekly receipt limit shown in the app.
DON'T
  • Scan receipts you found, were sent, or got from online groups.
  • Edit, crop out or fake anything on a receipt.
  • Scan a receipt twice or on two accounts. Fetch can take points back or ban you.

STUCK?HELP DESK

My receipt was rejected

Retake the photo in better light with the store name, date and total visible. If the receipt is older than the app allows, or was already scanned, it can't be accepted.

I hit a game milestone but got no points

Points can take a while. Close and reopen Fetch, keep the game installed, and wait at least a day. If they still haven't arrived, contact Fetch support from the help section in the app.

My code doesn't work

Check you're on the official redeem page, signed in to the right account, and that you copied the code exactly. If it still fails, contact Fetch support from the app. They handle all reward codes.

Can the FNTD User Guide fix my Fetch account?

No. We can't see or change Fetch accounts, points or codes. Fetch support is the only place that can help.
