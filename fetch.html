Before you start

Fetch is a free rewards app. You collect points and swap them for gift cards, delivered as digital codes.

Under 18? Ask a parent or guardian first. Fetch has its own rules about who can make an account, so check them in the app, and have an adult help with sign-up and anything that involves receipts or email.

Only download Fetch from the official app stores. Copies from anywhere else can steal your account.

Step 1: Scan receipts safely

Almost any receipt earns points, from any store, even if you didn't buy a featured brand.

  1. Open Fetch and tap the scan button at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Lay the receipt flat in good light. Make sure the store name, date and total are all in the photo. Long receipts can be scanned in parts.
  3. Check the photo is sharp, then submit it. Points usually show up within a few minutes.
  4. Scan receipts soon after you shop. Fetch only accepts recent receipts, and the app shows the current time limit.

Do

  • Scan receipts from your own shopping, or your household's with permission.
  • Scan each receipt once, then throw it away or keep it somewhere safe.
  • Keep an eye on the weekly receipt limit shown in the app.

Don't

  • Scan receipts you found, were sent, or got from online groups.
  • Edit, crop out or fake anything on a receipt.
  • Scan the same receipt twice or from two accounts. Fetch can remove points or ban the account.

Step 2: Play featured games

Fetch Play gives you points for reaching milestones in games, like hitting a certain level. These are usually worth far more than a receipt.

  1. Open the Play tab in Fetch and pick a game.
  2. Install it from the link inside Fetch. Installing it any other way means your progress may not count.
  3. Open the game once from Fetch, then play as normal. Each milestone has a time limit, shown in the app.
  4. Points are added automatically when you reach a milestone. Keep the game installed until they arrive.

You never need to spend money in these games to reach a milestone. Points shown below are what we saw. Your app can offer different games and amounts, so it always has the final say.

Step 3: Redeem your digital codes

Once you have enough points, you can swap them for a gift card. It arrives as a digital code.

  1. Open the Rewards tab in Fetch and pick a gift card, like Roblox.
  2. Choose an amount and confirm. Fetch shows how many points it costs before you agree.
  3. Your code appears in Fetch, usually within a few minutes, and stays saved in the app.
  4. Redeem it on the gift card's official site. For Roblox, sign in and go to roblox.com/redeem.

Keep your code secret. Anyone who sees it can use it. No real staff member, YouTuber or Discord mod will ever ask for your code, and sites promising "free Robux" are scams.

Something went wrong?

My receipt was rejected

Retake the photo in better light with the store name, date and total visible. If the receipt is older than the app allows, or was already scanned, it can't be accepted.

I reached a game milestone but got no points

Points can take a while to arrive. Close and reopen Fetch, keep the game installed, and wait at least a day. If they still haven't arrived, contact Fetch support from the help section in the app.

My code doesn't work

Check you're on the official redeem page and signed in to the right account, and that you copied the code exactly. If it still fails, contact Fetch support from the app. They handle all reward codes.

Can the FNTD User Guide fix my Fetch account?

No. We can't see or change Fetch accounts, points or codes. Fetch support is the only place that can help.
